Frequently asked questions

Can I download an iOS installer from this site?

iPhone and iPad apps are not distributed as direct installer files on websites. This site does not provide an IPA download.

Can I add the website to my Home Screen?

In Safari, use Share, then choose 'Add to Home Screen' if that option is available on your device.

Where will processed files go?

Depending on the tool and browser, files may appear in Downloads or a location you choose in the Files app.
